Good visualization (https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/elena.cherednikova/viz/NordicDigitalDevelopmentSustainableProsperity/DigitalDashboard)

I would describe this visualization as a good and effective one because it is interactive and easy to read. Aesthetically, it is visually appealing and easy for the audience to follow. The visualization provides clear titles and labels, which help viewers understand the information being presented. From a perceptual perspective, the data are displayed clearly, making it easy for readers to compare values across different countries. What I like most about this visualization is that it allows users to select a country and compare it with others. It also provides additional details when a data point is clicked, which helps prevent the visualization from becoming overcrowded with text and keeps the audience focused on the key information. Substantively, the visualization presents the data accurately and effectively. Furthermore, it remains objective and neutral, as it does not use language or visual elements that might evoke strong emotions or bias the audience’s interpretation of the data.

Poor visualization (https://datavizproject.com/data-type/bar-chart/)

This visualization is engaging but not particularly effective at communicating the data. From a substantive perspective, the chart lacks a clear title, category labels, and context, which makes it difficult to understand what the percentages represent. From a perceptive perspective, the large animal illustrations overshadow parts of the bars and takes away audience’s attention from the quantitative information and makes hard for the readers to accurately compare values. The placement of some labels, particularly the 98%, 100%, and 90% also creates confusion. Aesthetically, the graphic is attractive and memorable, using appealing colors and illustrations that may capture audience interest. However, too much decoration dominates the chart and compete with the data for attention. Overall, while the visualization succeeds in grabbing audience’s attention, it is less successful as a data visualization since clarity and accurate interpretation are undermined for the sake of aesthetics.
